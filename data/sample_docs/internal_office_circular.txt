INTERNAL OFFICE CIRCULAR — DOCUMENT HANDLING PROCEDURE
(Synthetic sample document for demonstration purposes only — RESTRICTED)

Section 1 - Purpose
This circular sets out the internal procedure for handling, storing, and
disposing of case files within the department. It is marked
"restricted" in this demo to illustrate role-based access control: it
should be visible to "officer" and "admin" roles, but not to "public"
users of the retrieval system.

Section 2 - File Classification
All case files must be classified upon receipt as Routine, Priority, or
Time-Bound. Time-Bound files must be flagged in the tracking system with
a due date, and the assigned officer is responsible for escalating to
their supervisor if the file is at risk of missing its deadline.

Section 3 - Access Logging
Every access to a physical or digital case file must be logged with the
accessing officer's ID, timestamp, and purpose of access. Logs are
retained for a minimum of five (5) years and are subject to periodic
internal audit.

Section 4 - Escalation Procedure
Files pending beyond the prescribed timeline without valid justification
must be escalated to the next reporting level within three (3) working
days of the deadline lapsing. A written explanation must accompany the
escalation.

Section 5 - Disposal
Files may only be disposed of, archived, or digitized-and-destroyed
following the retention schedule published separately by the records
management unit. No file marked "under litigation" may be disposed of
under any circumstance until the litigation hold is formally lifted.
