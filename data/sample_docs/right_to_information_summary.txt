RIGHT TO INFORMATION — CITIZEN GUIDANCE SUMMARY
(Synthetic sample document for demonstration purposes only)

Section 1 - Purpose
This summary explains, in plain language, how citizens can request
information from public authorities. It is intended for educational and
demonstration use in a document-retrieval system and does not replace the
actual statute.

Section 2 - Who Can File a Request
Any citizen may file an information request with a public authority. No
reason needs to be given for the request. Requests may be filed in
writing or electronically through the authority's designated portal.

Section 3 - Fees
A nominal application fee is payable at the time of filing. Applicants
belonging to economically weaker sections, as defined by the relevant
state guidelines, are exempt from this fee. No fee is charged for
providing information related to the applicant's own life or personal
liberty.

Section 4 - Response Timelines
A public authority is required to respond to an information request
within thirty (30) days of receipt. If the request concerns the life or
liberty of a person, the authority must respond within forty-eight (48)
hours. If the request is transferred to another public authority because
it holds the relevant records, the response clock restarts from the date
of transfer, and the transferring authority must notify the applicant.

Section 5 - Grounds for Refusal
A request may be refused if the information falls under an exempted
category, including but not limited to: information affecting national
security, information that would impede an ongoing investigation, or
information that constitutes an invasion of personal privacy without a
larger public interest justification. Any refusal must be communicated
in writing, stating the specific exemption relied upon and the process
for filing an appeal.

Section 6 - First Appeal
If an applicant does not receive a response within the prescribed
timeline, or is dissatisfied with the response, they may file a first
appeal with the designated appellate officer within thirty (30) days of
the response (or of the deadline lapsing). The appellate officer must
dispose of the appeal within thirty (30) days, extendable by a further
fifteen (15) days for recorded reasons.

Section 7 - Second Appeal
If the applicant remains dissatisfied after the first appeal, a second
appeal may be filed with the relevant Information Commission within
ninety (90) days of the first appellate decision.

Section 8 - Penalties
An officer who, without reasonable cause, refuses to accept a request,
fails to respond within the prescribed period, or knowingly provides
incorrect or misleading information, may be liable for a penalty as
determined by the Information Commission.
