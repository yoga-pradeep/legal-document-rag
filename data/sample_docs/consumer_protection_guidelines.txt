CONSUMER PROTECTION — GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL GUIDELINES
(Synthetic sample document for demonstration purposes only)

Section 1 - Scope
These guidelines describe how consumers can seek redress for defective
goods, deficient services, and unfair trade practices. This is a
simplified educational summary, written for a document-retrieval demo,
and does not replace the actual statute or regulations.

Section 2 - Where to File a Complaint
Complaints may be filed with the District Consumer Disputes Redressal
Commission where the value of goods or services, along with compensation
claimed, does not exceed the prescribed district-level limit. Complaints
above that limit, up to the state-level limit, go to the State
Commission. Complaints above the state-level limit go to the National
Commission.

Section 3 - Who Can File
A complaint may be filed by the consumer, a registered consumer
association, one or more consumers on behalf of a larger group with
similar interests, or the central or state government.

Section 4 - Limitation Period
A complaint must be filed within two (2) years from the date on which
the cause of action arose. The Commission may condone a delay if
sufficient cause is shown in writing.

Section 5 - E-Commerce Obligations
E-commerce entities are required to display the total price of goods and
services, including all applicable charges, provide a clear return,
refund, and exchange policy, and appoint a grievance officer whose
contact details are published on the platform. Grievances must be
acknowledged within forty-eight (48) hours and resolved within one (1)
month of receipt.

Section 6 - Product Liability
A manufacturer, seller, or service provider may be held liable for harm
caused by a defective product or deficient service, including
manufacturing defects, design defects, or failure to provide adequate
instructions or warnings about associated risks.

Section 7 - Penalties for Misleading Advertisements
Any person who issues a false or misleading advertisement that is
prejudicial to consumer interest may be liable for a penalty, and
repeated violations may result in an extended penalty and a temporary
ban on endorsing the product or service in question.
